# Mementos Rules And Guidelines 
Mementos are a way for people to create art for the YNFGS on Violetproject, to have their work featured as a collectible on the site. In this document, the guidelines for all things related to mementos are here in this document.

The guidelines may be changed at any time if needed.

We reserve the right to remove some or all of someones art in the event of them exhibiting poor or undesired behavior. On the flip side, you reserve the right to have mementos you created removed from the site at any time.

## Creation of Mementos

Mementos are PNG and GIF format.
Mementos can currently be created for the following.
- Map ID (Entering a map)
- Map ID + Coordinates (Being within certain coordinates in a map)
- Switches being ON or OFF
- Variables being at a certain point (less than, equal too, >=, <=, not equal too, equal to, etc)
- Interacting with an event ID (i.e interacting with an npc)
- Collision with an event ID (i.e walking into a collision warp)

And any combination of the above (as multiple conditions (separate) or as one combined (i.e within coords with a certain var number together), or both)

Dimensions for mementos are typically 37x37 pixel art, but it is not a requirement. Art is displayed with image-rendering: pixelated; styling, so non pixel art may look different than expected on the site. 

Mementos must have some sort of representation of the condition they are for. Entirely unrelated art will not be accepted. Correlation with the condition is up to memento handlers discretion, but we welcome feedback with open arms.

Mementos may not be accepted or may be removed if...

- The creator of the ingame content requests so.
- The art is disrespectful.
- The art does not take serious topics seriously.
- The badge handlers decide so for other reasons that may come up.

Please feel free to give feedback if you think a decision made is unfair.

There is no limit to how many mementos can be for a single condition, nor how many mementos can be made by a single artist.

Mementos may take time to be added to the site, as we do not have the time to update it always. We are not required to add it to the site as soon as possible, or add it at all.

## Etiquette Surrounding Mementos

Rules must be followed no matter whether you are a memento artist or not. Refusal to follow these rules can lead to many consequences, from suspension of creating mementos, permanant ban from creating mementos, to full site/server wide bans and mutes.

Be respectful towards artists and their art. Do not trash on artists because you believe their art is "bad" or "poorly made". This is meant to be a space for everyone, and it is no place for discouragement purely because of your opinion on what art "should" be.

All site and server rules apply aswell.

### Have fun creating!
